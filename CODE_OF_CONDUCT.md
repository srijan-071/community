# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Keep discussions respectful, constructive, and focused on improving the project. Assume good intent, explain disagreements with evidence, and give maintainers and contributors space to respond.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, deliberate disruption, and sharing private information without permission.

## Reporting concerns

If a contribution or discussion violates these standards, report it privately to the repository maintainers with a link or description of the relevant content. Do not amplify harmful content while reporting it.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project constructive and safe.
